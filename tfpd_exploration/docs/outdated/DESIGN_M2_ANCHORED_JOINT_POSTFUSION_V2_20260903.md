# AJPF V2 successor design

V2 is an additive successor to the immutable V1 training failure.  It does
not alter, retry, or write below the V1 root.  V2 binds the V1 incident
descriptor and substitutes only the missing state-digest call before source
materialization or any optimizer step.

The V2 digest law is the reviewed scorer law: a holder whose sole public
attribute is `student` is passed to
`m2_postfusion_checkpoint_score_v1.physical._student_state_sha`.  This is
equivalent to the reviewed PIT tensor framing for the selected ASCII state
keys and is required to equal the frozen selected student digest
`2a340745e2e1b4c7eecb4b187c9b061548bf3686f12389aae76bf53e4f3acc20`.

The CPU-only strict-load source regression is an admission proof, not an
experiment endpoint.  After it and the held V1 incident witness, V2 executes
the unchanged shared AJPF experiment: one GPU0 materialization, the exact
three arms for twelve epochs, epoch12 sealing, then a fresh CPU-only
13-session/78-row scorer and the unchanged two gates.  The V2 route owns new
training and score roots, schemas, capability identity, and predecessor
receipt binding; it shares rather than copies the V1 runner/training/scorer
implementation.
